Before you send

What to put in the first message

A trip question gets answered in one pass when the message carries these five things. Without them, the first reply is just us asking for them, and that costs you a day.

  • Departure city or airport, and the dates in MM/DD/YYYY
  • How many days of leave you are actually working with
  • Party size, and who in it is willing to drive
  • The trip name in your Lodestar Atlas account, if the question is about a saved plan
  • A rough Ledger ceiling in US dollars, per person, per day

If it is a billing question

Send the email address the subscription is under and the last four digits of the card. Never send a full card number through this form or by email. We will not ask for one, and anyone who does is not us.

If it is a correction

Name the page, quote the line that is wrong, and link the source if you have one. Corrections that arrive with a source are usually live the same week, with a dated note on the page saying what changed.
