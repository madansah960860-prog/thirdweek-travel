Atlas Pro gives every trip a share link. Anyone holding it can read the Daylines, the travel legs and the Ledger. Editing is a separate switch you turn on per person, so a link pasted into a group chat stays read-only until you decide otherwise.

Every edit is stamped with who made it and when, and you can roll a Dayline back to any earlier state for 30 days.
