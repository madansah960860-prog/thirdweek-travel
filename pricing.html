Which plan fits, judged by how you actually travel

Most people overbuy software. Read the three cases below and take the smallest one that matches you. You can move up later without losing anything.

Stay on Free if

  • You take one or two trips a year
  • No trip runs past 14 days
  • You plan alone or with one other person
  • Printing the Daylines before you fly is enough

Move to Atlas Pro if

  • You are planning a third trip this year
  • Part of the route has no cell coverage
  • Three or more people need the same plan
  • You want lodging and fuel inside the Ledger

Buy a custom itinerary if

  • The trip has a date that cannot move
  • Permits or timed entries are involved
  • It is a first international trip with children
  • You would rather buy back the research hours

The one case where Free is the wrong answer

A long drive with no signal. If your route crosses a national park, a rural highway or a mountain pass, the plan you can read is the plan on paper or in the Field Export. Free prints fine from a browser, so print it before you leave. If you would rather not, that single trip is the case for Atlas Pro.

When a person should plot it instead

Some trips are worth handing to a person. An anniversary, a first international trip with children, a two-week route with too many moving parts to hold in your head. That is what this service is for.

The four stages

  1. You file the brief

    Dates, departure airport, budget, party, mobility, and what actually matters to you. A short form, about ten minutes.

  2. We research and plot it

    Five to seven business days, checked against current opening hours, permit windows and seasonal closures.

  3. One revision call

    Thirty minutes on video. We change what is wrong and rebuild every Dayline the change touches.

  4. You take delivery

    An editable trip plus a print-ready Field Export, and three months of Atlas Pro so you can keep moving things.

Billing, answered directly

Is there a free trial of Atlas Pro? No. The Free tier is the trial, and it does not expire.

Can I switch between monthly and annual? Yes, at any renewal. A mid-term switch is prorated to the day.

Do prices change? If we raise the price, existing subscribers hold their rate for at least twelve months and hear from us by email at least thirty days beforehand.

How do I cancel? Two clicks on your account page. No email to write, no retention call to sit through.
