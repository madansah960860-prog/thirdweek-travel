Where the unbilled hours actually go

The agents we talk to lose most of their unpaid time to the same three tasks: rebuilding a day plan in a word processor, working out drive times by hand, and reformatting the whole document because a client moved one night.

Templates from trips you already sold

Save any plan as a template. The Tuscany week you have sold eleven times starts as a plotted route instead of a blank page, and editing the template leaves everything you already sent untouched.

Output a client can actually read

A Field Export carrying your logo, your colors and your contact block in the footer. Or a live link that shows your revisions as you make them, with no attachment to re-send.

One change, no re-typing

Move a stop and True Pace re-measures the legs while the Ledger re-totals the day and the trip. No arithmetic by hand, and no argument about which version of the PDF is current.

What the agent plans add

Your brand on the Field Export

Your logo, your colors, your agency name and contact block. On the White Label tier every trace of Lodestar Atlas is removed, and nothing your client receives carries our marketing.

Every client in one workspace

Each trip is filed under a client record with notes, dates and status. Filter by departure date to see exactly what needs attention this week and what can wait.

Seats for the rest of the team

Colleagues get their own logins. Trips can be assigned, shared internally and handed over cleanly when someone is on leave, with the note history intact.

Notes the client never sees

Attach a private note to any stop: confirmation numbers, supplier contacts, the reason you chose that hotel over the one down the road. None of it reaches the client-facing document.

Bulk export by departure window

Generate the Field Export for every trip leaving inside a date range in one pass. That is the difference between an hour and a morning in the week before a group departure.

Your client list stays yours

We insert no affiliate links into your documents, we never contact your clients, and we do not mine your itineraries to build the public Atlas. Nothing you plan here becomes our inventory.

What this is not

Lodestar Atlas is planning software. It is not a booking engine, a GDS, a CRM, an accounting system or a payment processor. We do not hold client funds, issue tickets or act as a seller of travel. Keep the systems you already run; this sits alongside them and does the measuring.

How onboarding runs

  1. A 30-minute demo call with one of us, not a sales team.
  2. A 14-day trial workspace with your branding already applied.
  3. We rebuild up to three of your existing itineraries as templates at no charge.
  4. You decide. If it is not right, delete the workspace and nothing is charged.
Exit terms

If you leave, here is what you take

Software you run a business on should be easy to walk away from. These terms are the same on every agent tier, and they are written into the contract rather than left to goodwill.

Your data, in a usable format

Request a full export at any time and get every client record, trip, Dayline, supplier note and Ledger line as JSON, plus a Field Export PDF of every trip. No exit fee and no waiting period.

Client links keep working

Live links you already sent stay readable for 90 days after a cancellation, so a client traveling next month is not left holding a dead URL. After that they expire; the PDFs your clients downloaded never do.

The templates are yours

Itineraries you built here are your work product. We claim no license to resell, republish or fold them into the public Atlas, during the subscription or after it ends.
