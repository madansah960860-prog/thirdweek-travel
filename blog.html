What every guide here assumes

You are departing from a US airport. You have somewhere between 10 and 20 vacation days for the year, not six weeks. Costs are in USD, distances in miles, temperatures in °F, and dates in MM/DD/YYYY.

Where a guide cites a price, a permit rule or a lead time, that figure is the one we could verify on the update date printed at the top of the article. Prices move. Check anything you are about to spend money on.

How these get written

Every guide is written by someone here who has taken the trip or plotted it for a client. It is then checked against official sources before it goes up.

Each article carries a published date and an updated date. We move the updated date when a fact changes, never on a schedule to look current.

If a guide carries affiliate links, it says so at the top of that page, not only in the footer.
