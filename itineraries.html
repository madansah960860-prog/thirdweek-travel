Finished plans you can take apart

These are not highlight lists. Every day here is a Dayline: stops with a start time, the measured travel time between them, and a Ledger total in dollars. Open one, copy it, change anything that does not fit.

Read the three long plans Start from an empty page instead

Every plan lists what the Ledger leaves out, not only what it covers.

What every plan carries

Stops with start times

Each time is set around opening hours, light and crowd patterns. Convenience comes second.

Measured legs, not guesses

Driving, walking or transit, with distance in miles. True Pace checks that the day still fits inside a day.

A Ledger for each day

In USD, for a stated party size, with a plain list of what the number leaves out.

The part guides leave out

What to book ahead, what to cut when you are short on time, and the point where the plan usually breaks.

What a sample plan is, and what it is not

It is a worked example, not a booking. Nothing here is reserved in your name. Prices were checked on the day the plan was written and they drift; opening hours, road status and shuttle systems change without notice. Treat every figure as a starting estimate and confirm it with the operator before you pay anything. Each plan is also written for one stated party size and season, so if yours differ, the Ledger will differ too. See our disclaimer.

Six shorter plans, in outline

These load straight into the planner with their stops and Daylines already plotted. The outlines below tell you the shape of each trip before you spend time opening it.

Lisbon Long Weekend · 4 days

  • Portugal
  • 2 travelers
  • $392 activities

Built around a Thursday-night departure from the US East Coast and a Monday-night return. It costs two PTO days. The first Dayline is deliberately thin, because you land in the morning with no sleep behind you.

Outline: Alfama and the miradouros on foot, a full day in Belem and the Time Out Market, a Sintra day trip on a timed ticket, then a last morning in Principe Real before the airport.

Big Sur Coastal Drive · 3 days

  • California
  • 2 travelers
  • $268 activities

Ninety miles of Highway 1, driven north to south so the ocean sits on your right and every pullout is a right turn. That one decision improves the trip more than any stop on it.

Outline: Carmel down to Bixby Bridge, Point Lobos and Pfeiffer Beach timed to low tide, McWay Falls in the last hour of light, then out through Cambria.

Banff and the Icefields Parkway · 5 days

  • Alberta, Canada
  • 4 travelers
  • $1,140 activities

Two nights in Banff, one on the parkway, two in Jasper. The Moraine Lake shuttle reservation is the fixed point here, and every other Dayline is built to fit around it.

Outline: Lake Louise at sunrise, Moraine Lake by shuttle, Peyto and Bow Lake, the Athabasca Glacier, and Maligne Canyon on the last morning.

New Orleans · 3 days

  • Louisiana
  • 2 travelers
  • $486 activities

A long weekend that spends most of its hours outside the French Quarter, because that is where the city is most itself. Two dinners are reserved before you fly.

Outline: the Garden District on foot, a Whitney Plantation visit, Frenchmen Street after dark, a swamp tour, and beignets timed to the quiet hour.

Dolomites Hiking Base · 6 days

  • Northern Italy
  • 2 travelers
  • $744 activities

One base in Val Gardena and six day hikes that get harder as the week goes on, with a rest Dayline in the middle that most plans refuse to include. Trailhead parking fills before 8 AM, so every start time is early.

Outline: the Seceda ridgeline, Alpe di Siusi, the Tre Cime loop, Lago di Braies at dawn, a rifugio lunch, and a cable car day where the legs get a break.

Mexico City for Eaters · 4 days

  • Mexico
  • 2 travelers
  • $418 activities

Plotted around meals rather than sights, with museums placed in the gaps between them. The first Dayline is gentle on purpose: the city sits at 7,350 feet and altitude slows most visitors arriving from sea level.

Outline: a Roma and Condesa market walk, the Museo Nacional de Antropologia, Coyoacan on a Saturday, a Teotihuacan half day, and one tasting dinner reserved in advance.
