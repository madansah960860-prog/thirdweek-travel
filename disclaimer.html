1. We are a planning tool, not a travel provider

Lodestar Atlas LLC publishes travel information, maintains the Atlas of destinations and stops, and provides itinerary-planning software. We are not a travel agency, tour operator, air carrier, accommodation provider, transport provider or seller of travel. We do not make bookings, take reservations, issue tickets or hold customer funds for suppliers.

Any booking you make is a contract directly between you and that supplier, on the supplier's terms. Cancellations, delays, overbooking, refunds, service quality and safety at a supplier's premises are matters between you and that supplier. We have no authority to resolve them and no liability for them.

2. Travel information changes constantly

Opening hours, entry fees, timed-entry systems, permit lotteries, shuttle operations, seasonal closures, road conditions, visa rules, currency exchange rates and public safety conditions all change frequently and often without notice.

We research carefully and update our material on a rolling basis, and we still expect that some of it is out of date at any given moment. You are responsible for verifying anything that matters with an official source before you travel. For the destinations we cover, that usually means:

  • the National Park Service and recreation.gov for US parks, permits and campgrounds;
  • the individual park, temple, museum or operator's own website for hours and fees;
  • the US Department of State country information pages for entry requirements and travel advisories;
  • the destination's national meteorological service and road authority for weather and road status;
  • your airline and your accommodation for their own current terms.

3. Cost figures are estimates, not quotes

Every price, budget table and daily total on this site, including every figure the Ledger produces, is a planning estimate expressed in US dollars. Estimates are useful for comparing options within a plan. They are not offers, not quotes, and not a promise that you will pay that amount.

Actual costs vary with your dates, party size, booking timing, currency movements, taxes and local fees. Figures marked as placeholders on this site have not yet been verified and must be replaced with verified information before the site is used commercially.

4. Safety information is general, not personal advice

Our guides include safety notes because omitting them would be worse. They are general information written for a general audience. They are not a risk assessment for your trip, your fitness, your experience level or the conditions on the day. A Dayline that True Pace marks as comfortable is a statement about distance and driving time, not about whether the day is safe for you.

Hiking, driving on unfamiliar roads, swimming, water sports, glacier travel, high-altitude activity and remote travel carry real risk of injury and death. Conditions change hourly. You are responsible for assessing your own capability, checking current conditions, carrying appropriate equipment and turning back when that is the right call. Where a licensed guide is recommended by local authorities, use one.

5. Not professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, tax, insurance or medical advice.

  • Immigration and visas. Requirements depend on your citizenship, your route and current policy. Consult the relevant government authority.
  • Insurance. We are not an insurance broker or advisor. Read any policy's terms, limits and exclusions yourself before buying.
  • Health. Vaccination requirements, altitude, heat and pre-existing conditions are matters for a qualified medical professional who knows your history.
  • Money. Card fees, exchange rates and foreign transaction terms are matters for your bank.

6. Third-party links

We link to external websites for verification, booking and further reading. Some of those are affiliate links, disclosed on the page where they appear and in our Affiliate Disclosure. We do not control third-party sites and are not responsible for their content, accuracy, availability, terms or privacy practices. A link is not an endorsement of everything on the destination site.

7. Reviews and testimonials

Testimonials on this site describe the experience of specific individuals and are not a guarantee that you will have a similar result. Our full collection and publication practice is set out on the reviews page. Items marked as placeholders have not been verified and must be replaced or removed before launch.

8. Photography

Photographs on this site are used to illustrate the character of a destination or a theme. They are not necessarily taken at the exact location named nearby, on the date named, or in the conditions you will encounter. Conditions in a photograph are not a representation of what you will see.
