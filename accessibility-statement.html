Accessibility Statement: where we stand and what still needs work

Last updated 08/28/2026. Last audited 08/21/2026. What meets WCAG 2.1 level AA today, what does not yet, how we test it, and how to tell us when something blocks you.

Our commitment. Lodestar Atlas aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1, level AA, across this entire website, the planner and every Dayline in it included.

1. Conformance status

This site is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Partially conformant means most of the content meets the standard, and some parts do not yet. We publish the known gaps in section 4 rather than claiming full conformance, because an honest statement is more useful to you than an optimistic one.

2. What we have implemented

Structure and navigation

  • Semantic HTML landmarks on every page: header, nav, main, aside and footer.
  • A skip-to-content link that is the first focusable element on every page.
  • One h1 per page, with headings in logical order and no levels skipped.
  • Breadcrumbs on every interior page, marked up as a labeled navigation region.
  • Descriptive page titles and link text. No link reads only "click here" or "read more".

Keyboard

  • Every interactive element is reachable and operable with a keyboard alone.
  • Visible focus indicators with a 3px outline and 3px offset, never removed.
  • Menus and accordions use native buttons with correct aria-expanded state.
  • Escape closes the mobile menu and any open dropdown, returning focus to the trigger.
  • No keyboard traps anywhere on the site.

Visual design

  • Body text is 17px with a 1.8 line height, and the site remains usable at 200 percent zoom.
  • All body text meets or exceeds a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background, and large text exceeds 3:1. Placeholder text is held to the same 4.5:1 standard as body text.
  • Color is never the only way information is conveyed. Errors carry an icon and text, not just a red border.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels with at least 8px of spacing.
  • No horizontal scrolling at any width from 320px upward.

Images and media

  • Every content image has descriptive alt text. Decorative shapes are CSS only and marked aria-hidden.
  • Width and height attributes on every image so nothing shifts as the page loads.
  • No autoplaying audio or video anywhere on the site.

Forms

  • Every field has a visible label positioned above it. Placeholders are never used as labels.
  • Errors appear inline beneath the field they belong to, described in words, and are announced to screen readers through a live region.
  • Invalid fields are marked with aria-invalid and focus moves to the first one on a failed submission.
  • Autocomplete attributes are set on name, email and phone fields.

Motion

  • All animation is decorative and enhances content that is already visible. No content is hidden behind an animation that might not fire.
  • When your system requests reduced motion, every transition, scroll reveal, image zoom and the drifting leaf motif is disabled.
  • Nothing flashes more than three times per second.
  • No parallax, no scroll hijacking, and no motion required to complete any task.

3. How we test

  • Automated checks on every page as part of our release process.
  • Manual keyboard-only walkthroughs of the planner, the Ledger, all forms, the navigation and the cookie banner.
  • Screen reader testing with NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on macOS and iOS.
  • Zoom testing at 200 and 400 percent, and at viewport widths of 360, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 pixels.
  • Contrast verification of every text and background token pairing against the WCAG formula.
  • Testing with reduced-motion and increased-contrast system settings enabled.

5. Compatibility

This site is designed to work with current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile, combined with the assistive technologies listed above. It should degrade gracefully in older browsers, though some visual polish will be missing. JavaScript improves the experience but the content, navigation and forms remain readable and usable without it.

6. Reporting a barrier

Tell us what happened and we will fix it. Please include the page address, what you were trying to do, and what assistive technology and browser you were using if you know.

7. Getting the same information another way

If any content here is not accessible to you, we will provide it in another format at no cost. That includes reading an itinerary to you over the phone, sending a plain-text version of any guide, or emailing a large-print PDF. Ask at access@lodestaratlas.com.
