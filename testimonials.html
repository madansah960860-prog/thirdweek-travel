Reviews, with the three-star ones left in

We ask two weeks after the trip ends, not at signup, and we publish what comes back. The three and four star reviews are on this page with the rest.

Read the reviews How they are collected

Edited for length and typos only. Never for tone.

Requested after the trip ends

The request goes out two weeks after the return date. Not at signup, and not while someone is still excited about a plan they have not travelled yet.

Nothing offered in exchange

No discounts, credits, prize entries or free months for writing one. A review here bought the writer nothing at all.

The critical ones stay up

Three-star reviews sit in the same grid as the five-star ones, with our reply underneath where a reply is owed.

Tied to a real account

Every review maps to an account with a completed trip on it. Anonymous submissions are not published.

Twelve reviews, in the order they arrived

Two quarters of responses, unsorted and unfiltered. Plan and date are shown next to each name.

Acted on

What the three and four star reviews changed

A complaint that does not move anything is just a comment. Here is where the critical reviews on this page landed, including the one we have not fixed.

Thin Atlas coverage

Shipped. Costa Rica was rebuilt from scratch in July and now carries 34 verified stops. Every Atlas entry has since been given a last-checked date you can see before you use it.

Lodging estimates set too low

Shipped. The lodging line in the Ledger now shows a seasonal range rather than a single average, so a peak-season week reads as a peak-season week.

No drag to reorder on a phone

Not fixed. Reordering a Dayline on a small screen is still move-up and move-down buttons. Dragging is on the list and has been for two quarters. We would rather say that than imply it is coming next month.

How this page is put together

Every review here comes from a Lodestar Atlas account holder who finished a trip planned in the product. The request goes out by email two weeks after the end date, once, with no reminder and no follow-up.

What gets edited

Length, obvious typos, and anything that would identify a third party such as a named hotel employee. Tone is left alone. Criticism is not softened and nothing is reworded to read better.

What we will not do

  • Offer discounts, credits, free months or prize entries in exchange for a review.
  • Write reviews ourselves, or ask staff, family or contractors to write them.
  • Pull a review for being critical. Removal happens only for abuse, doxxing or a legal demand.
  • Show an aggregate star rating anywhere on this site. Twelve reviews is not a sample you can honestly average.

If one of these looks fake to you

Write to hello@lodestaratlas.com with the reviewer's name. We re-check it against the account record and tell you what we find, whichever way it goes.

These commitments follow the FTC guidance on endorsements and testimonials. An incentivized review would be labeled as one. None on this page were.

Send us yours

If you planned a trip here and have already taken it, we want the write-up, and we want the part that did not work more than the part that did.

Use the contact form and choose "Something else", or reply to the review request that landed in your inbox.
