The method

Four steps from a rough idea to a Field Export

You do not need dates, a hotel, or even a destination to begin. Lodestar Atlas works backward from whatever you already know.

  1. State what you have

    Days off, a rough budget, your departure airport, who is coming. Two minutes of input.

  2. Pull stops from the Atlas

    The Atlas is our researched stop library. Take what fits, type in your own, drag them into days.

  3. Read True Pace

    True Pace measures the travel time between every pair of stops and flags any day packed too tight to finish.

  4. Carry the Field Export

    Print the plan, or take the Field Export, a PDF that still reads when your phone has no signal.

Each day is a Dayline

A Dayline holds timed stops, the measured travel between them, and an honest daily total from the Ledger. Below are Days 1 to 3 of the Iceland South Coast plan, priced for two travelers.

Day 1

Golden Circle at half speed

Short first leg
  • 8:00 AM Breakfast in Reykjavík Coffee and a cardamom bun. Collect the rental car after this, not before it. Drive 45 min · 30 miles
  • 10:00 AM Þingvellir National Park The boardwalk runs down a rift valley between two tectonic plates. Parking is paid at the machine. Drive 50 min · 37 miles
  • 1:00 PM Geysir geothermal field Strokkur erupts every 6 to 10 minutes, so the wait is short. Lunch at the visitor center is adequate and no better. Drive 12 min · 6 miles
  • 3:00 PM Gullfoss waterfall Upper platform for the full width of the falls. Take the lower path only if it is free of ice. Drive 1 hr 5 min · 45 miles
  • 5:30 PM Secret Lagoon soak Quieter than the famous lagoon near the airport, and roughly a third of the price.
Estimated day cost
$186 for 2 travelers, excludes lodging
Day 2

Two waterfalls, then black sand

Longest drive
  • 8:30 AM Seljalandsfoss The path goes behind the water. A waterproof shell is not optional here. Drive 20 min · 17 miles
  • 10:30 AM Skógafoss 527 steps to the clifftop view. Skip the climb if the wind reads above 30 mph. Drive 35 min · 22 miles
  • 12:30 PM Lunch in Vík Last dependable groceries before the eastern stretch. Fill the gas tank at the same stop. Drive 10 min · 5 miles
  • 2:00 PM Reynisfjara black sand beach Sneaker waves here are genuinely dangerous. Stay well back from the waterline. Drive 1 hr 50 min · 87 miles
  • 5:30 PM Check in near Kirkjubæjarklaustur Small town, very few rooms. Book this night before anything else on the trip.
Estimated day cost
$142 for 2 travelers, excludes lodging
Day 3

Out to the glacier lagoon and back

Heaviest day
  • 8:00 AM Drive to Jökulsárlón Leave early. This is the single longest drive in the plan. Drive 1 hr 40 min · 84 miles
  • 10:00 AM Glacier lagoon boat tour Amphibious boats run May through October. Book the slot about two weeks ahead. Walk 8 min · 0.4 miles
  • 12:00 PM Diamond Beach Ice on black sand, directly across the road. Fifteen minutes covers it. Drive 55 min · 40 miles
  • 2:00 PM Svartífoss hike, Skaftafell 3.5 miles round trip, 400 feet of climb, basalt columns stacked behind the falls. Drive 2 hr 40 min · 152 miles
  • 7:00 PM Return to Reykjavík Refuel before the ring road turns inland. Stations thin out badly after dark.
Estimated day cost
$318 for 2 travelers, excludes lodging

Known limits

Four things Lodestar Atlas will not do for you

An instrument is only useful if you know what it does not measure. Read this before you start, not after.

It does not book anything

No flight, room, car or tour is reserved through us. Lodestar Atlas produces the plan; you book each line of it with the airline, hotel or operator directly.

It does not price live inventory

The Ledger works from researched typical costs, not from today's fares. Treat every figure as a planning estimate and confirm it at the point of sale.

It does not predict weather

True Pace measures distance and time, not conditions. A closed mountain pass or a storm off the coast will still rearrange your day, and no planner changes that.

And it does not replace advice you are owed. We are a planning tool, not a travel agency or tour operator. For visas, entry requirements, vaccination rules and travel insurance, go to the official source for your passport and destination. Our disclaimer sets out exactly where our responsibility ends.
