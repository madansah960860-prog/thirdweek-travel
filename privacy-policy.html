Privacy Policy: what we hold and why

Last updated 08/28/2026. Effective 09/01/2026. This page is the full record of the personal information Lodestar Atlas holds, the reason we hold each piece, how long it stays, and the routes you can use to change or delete it.

Please note. This policy is a starting template written for a US audience. It is not legal advice, and it should be reviewed and adapted by a licensed attorney before this site operates commercially.

Who we are. Lodestar Atlas LLC, 2120 Westlake Avenue N, Suite 300, Seattle, WA 98109, United States. Email privacy@lodestaratlas.com. Phone +1 (206) 555-0172. We are the controller of the personal information described here.

The plain-English version

We hold your account email, the trips and Daylines you build, and whatever you send us through the contact form. Server logs are deleted after 30 days. Analytics and advertising cookies are set only if you accept them, and we honor the Global Privacy Control signal. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

This summary is written for speed of reading. The numbered sections below are the operative text, and they govern where the two differ.

1. What we collect

We collect only what the service needs to work, plus what you choose to give us.

Information you give us directly

  • Account information: your email address and a password hash. A display name if you provide one.
  • Trip content: the destinations, stops, dates, notes and party size you enter into your Daylines in the planner.
  • Contact form submissions: your name, email address, optional phone number, the topic you select and your message.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address and the date you confirmed it.
  • Custom itinerary briefs: travel dates, departure airport, budget range, party composition, accessibility needs you choose to disclose, and preferences.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by our payment processor. We receive the last four digits of the card, the card brand, the billing country and the transaction result. We never see or store the full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, browser user agent, referring page, the page requested and a timestamp. Collected for security, abuse prevention and diagnosing errors.
  • Device and usage data: screen size, language and general interaction data, collected only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Local storage: your cookie choice, and if you have started one, an unsaved trip plan held in your own browser.

What we do not collect

We do not collect precise geolocation, biometric data, government identifiers, or health information. We do not buy personal information from data brokers, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

2. How we use it

  • To run the planner, including True Pace and the Ledger, and to save your trips.
  • To create and secure your account, and to authenticate you.
  • To process subscription payments and issue receipts.
  • To answer your messages and deliver the custom itinerary service you have purchased.
  • To send the email newsletter, if you asked for it.
  • To understand which guides need updating, using aggregate analytics where you have consented.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your trip content for advertising, and we do not use it to build the Atlas, our public destination and stop library, without asking you first.

3. Cookies and tracking

On your first visit we show a consent banner with three choices: accept all, reject non-essential, or manage preferences by category. Until you choose, we set only strictly necessary cookies.

  • Strictly necessary. Your consent choice, session security and form protection. These cannot be switched off because the site does not function without them.
  • Analytics. Aggregate page views and navigation paths. Set only if you accept.
  • Advertising. Third-party advertising cookies, including Google advertising cookies, used to serve and measure ads. Set only if you accept.

You can change your choice at any time from the Cookie Policy page, which lists each cookie, its purpose and its lifetime. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though blocking strictly necessary cookies will break parts of the site.

We honor the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser sends GPC, we treat it as an opt out of sale and sharing for that browser.

5. Google advertising cookies and how to opt out

We do not currently display advertising anywhere on this site. The advertising cookie category exists in our consent banner so that nothing can be set without your permission if that changes. If we do introduce advertising, it will appear only on content pages in clearly labeled slots, and never on legal pages, on the error page, or inside the trip planner. The rest of this section explains how third-party advertising cookies would work, and how to opt out of personalized advertising generally.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting optout.aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu if you are in Europe.
  • You may also opt out of Network Advertising Initiative member cookies at optout.networkadvertising.org.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will stop seeing ads. It means the ads you see are less likely to be based on your browsing history.

6. When we share information

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • With the processors listed above, under written contracts limiting their use of the data.
  • With people you choose to share a trip link with. A shared trip link exposes the trip content to anyone holding the link.
  • To comply with law, when we receive a valid legal request. Where we are permitted to tell you, we will.
  • To protect rights and safety, including investigating fraud or abuse.
  • In a business transfer, if the company is acquired or merges. You would be notified by email before your information moved to a new controller.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

8. Security

We use TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest for stored trip content, hashed passwords, access controls limiting employee access to what their role requires, and regular dependency updates. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable state and federal law.

9. Children's privacy and COPPA

This service is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. Account creation requires you to be at least 13, and purchasing a subscription requires you to be at least 18 or to have a parent or guardian make the purchase.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, email privacy@lodestaratlas.com and we will delete it promptly. Parents and guardians may contact us at the same address to review or delete information about their child.

10. California rights: CCPA and CPRA, and Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. The categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete. Deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions such as completing a transaction or complying with a legal obligation.
  • Right to correct. Correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. See below.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the statute.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny you service, charge a different price or provide a different quality of service because you exercised a privacy right.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt out is technically necessary for us to stop, because we do not start. You may nevertheless submit a formal opt-out request, and we will record it and confirm in writing.

To submit a request:

  • Email privacy@lodestaratlas.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share".
  • Or use the contact form and select "Privacy or data request".
  • Or call +1 (206) 555-0172 during business hours.

We do not require an account to submit a request. We verify requests by asking you to confirm from the email address on file, or by asking for two pieces of information matching our records if you do not have an account. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with written permission that we can verify.

We acknowledge requests within 10 business days and complete them within 45 calendar days, extendable once by a further 45 days where reasonably necessary, in which case we will tell you why.

Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers such as email address and IP address; commercial information such as subscription and transaction records; internet activity such as page views where consented; and geolocation inferred only to the level of country from an IP address. Categories sold or shared in the last 12 months: none.

11. Note for visitors in the EU and UK

Our service is aimed at travelers in the United States, but we welcome visitors from anywhere. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the GDPR or UK GDPR may apply to our processing of your personal information.

Our legal bases are: performance of a contract for account and subscription data; consent for analytics cookies, advertising cookies and the newsletter; and legitimate interests for security logging and fraud prevention, balanced against your rights.

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

Personal information is processed and stored in the United States. Where we transfer personal information out of the EEA or UK we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with additional safeguards where required.

12. Email and CAN-SPAM

Our marketing emails comply with the CAN-SPAM Act. Every marketing email we send:

  • identifies Lodestar Atlas LLC as the sender, with a truthful subject line and header information;
  • includes our physical mailing address, 2120 Westlake Avenue N, Suite 300, Seattle, WA 98109, United States;
  • includes a clear one-click unsubscribe link that works for at least 30 days after sending;
  • and is honored within 10 business days of your unsubscribe, in practice immediately.

Newsletter subscriptions are confirmed opt-in: you are not subscribed until you click the link in the confirmation email. Transactional emails such as receipts, password resets and custom itinerary delivery are not marketing and are sent regardless of newsletter status.
